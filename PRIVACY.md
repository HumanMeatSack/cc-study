# CC Study privacy

Applies to CC Study 1.0 beta, November 23, 2026.

The app stores selected answers, attempt counts, per-question progress and the active question in its own local SQLite database. These records are used to resume practice and choose review questions. No study history is transmitted by the app.

This build has no Internet permission, account system, analytics, advertising SDK, microphone, camera or location access. It performs no background collection. If you choose an official-source button, Android opens the link in your browser; that browser and destination site have their own privacy practices.

Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. Android/vendor device-to-device transfer behavior may vary. Uninstalling the app clears its local data. There is no cloud account or cloud deletion request to manage.

Downloading from GitHub is separate from using the app and is subject to GitHub's own privacy policy. Feedback posted in this repository's Issues is public; avoid posting personal data or screenshots containing it.

Privacy questions may be filed through this repository's Issues page.
